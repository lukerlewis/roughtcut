Split the video into its main sections: usually 3 to 6, none shorter than about 45 seconds, and none at all for a video under 2 minutes. At the first word of each section after the intro, add a title card naming it in 2 to 5 words, with add_graphic and a graphic from the Titles group (list_graphics; one of Luke's own from Yours first if one fits), held about 2.5 seconds. Use the same graphic for every chapter so they read as a set.
