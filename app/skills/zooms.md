name: Zoom in on the key moments
on: false
---
Zoom in on the 2 to 5 lines that matter most (the hook, a key result, the takeaway), at 115 to 130%, centred on Luke's face. A zoom moves over the words you give it and stays there until the next zoom: zoom_in over the first word or two of the line (longer for a slow push), then zoom_out over a word or two after the line ends. Never two zooms within 20 seconds, and none on lines that have a graphic.
